Hunza Valley

Discover the breathtaking wonders of Hunza Valley

Rakaposhi Viewpoint

Rakaposhi is one of the most magnificent mountains in the world, standing at 7,788 meters above sea level. The viewpoint offers a stunning panoramic view of this snow-capped giant that dominates the Hunza skyline. Visitors travel from across the globe just to witness the breathtaking beauty of Rakaposhi reflected in the calm surrounding landscape.

Attabad Lake

Attabad Lake is a stunning turquoise lake formed in 2010 after a massive landslide blocked the Hunza River. Stretching over 21 kilometers, its brilliant blue-green waters are a sight to behold against the rocky mountain backdrop. Today it is one of Pakistan's most popular tourist destinations, offering boating and sightseeing experiences.

Baltit Fort

Baltit Fort is an ancient fortress that has stood for over 700 years, overlooking the Hunza Valley from its elevated position above Karimabad. It served as the residence of the Mirs of Hunza and is a remarkable example of Tibetan-influenced architecture. Restored and declared a UNESCO heritage site, it remains one of the most iconic landmarks in northern Pakistan.
